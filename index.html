World clock, time zone converter & 9–5 checker

Meridian is a free world clock and time zone converter. Compare local times in different cities, see UTC offsets and daylight-saving abbreviations such as EST, EDT, CET and CEST, or enter a date and time to plan ahead.

Add places to check which are within 09:00–17:00 local time. Working-hours labels update with live time or your chosen moment. The simple 9–5 rule applies every day and does not account for weekends, holidays or individual schedules.

Rotate the interactive 3D Earth to explore time zones, sunlight, twilight and city lights. Meridian works on desktop and mobile, without an account. Location access is optional and processed in your browser.
